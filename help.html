Stack. — Help & Support

Quick answers for gameplay, purchases, ads, and account data. Last updated: July 2026.

Contact support

Email stacks.support@gmail.com. Include your device model, iOS version, and a screenshot if something looks wrong.

Getting started

  • Choose a mode on the play screen, optionally arm power ups, then tap PLAY.
  • Tap, click, or press spacebar to drop the moving block.
  • Perfect drops build combos and earn more coins.

Coins & gems

  • Coins are earned by playing. Spend them on themes and effects in the Shop.
  • Gems are premium currency for power ups and revives. Buy gem packs in the Shop.
  • Daily free coins appear in the Shop when available (one claim per day).

Purchases

  • All purchases go through Apple In-App Purchase.
  • Gem and coin packs are consumable — once delivered, they cannot be restored like a subscription.
  • If you were charged but did not receive gems or coins, email support with your App Store receipt.

Rewarded ads

  • Watch a short ad to revive a run, double run coins, or claim a daily bonus when offered.
  • Ads need a stable internet connection. If an ad fails to load, try again on Wi‑Fi.
  • You can limit ad tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Friends & display names

  • Open Settings → Friends to set your display name and add friends with a friend code.
  • Offensive or inappropriate names are blocked automatically.
  • Friend codes share a snapshot of scores — they are stored on your device.

Rank & leaderboard

  • The Rank tab shows friends leaderboard and your personal bests.
  • Global rankings are a preview pool until cloud leaderboard ships in a future update.

Data & privacy

  • Game progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app removes local data.
  • Read our Privacy Policy for details on ads, analytics, and purchases.
