Privacy Policy

Stack. (the “App”) is operated by Khizer Mahmud. Last updated: July 2026.

This policy describes how we handle information when you play Stack. on iOS or the web demo.

Summary

Stack. stores most game data on your device. We do not require an account for basic play. Optional analytics and ads may collect limited technical data as described below.

Data stored on your device

  • Game progress: scores, coins, gems, inventory, themes, audio settings
  • Display name and friends list (if you use social features)
  • Anonymous analytics identifier (if an analytics endpoint is configured)

This data stays in your browser or app storage unless you enable a future cloud sync feature.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may provide purchase confirmation metadata required to deliver gems or coins.

Advertising

The App may show rewarded ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data per Google’s Privacy Policy. You may see an App Tracking Transparency prompt on iOS if personalized ads are enabled.

Analytics

If enabled, we collect anonymous events (sessions, shop views, runs) to improve the game. We do not intentionally collect email, precise location, or contact lists.

Children

Stack. is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Delete the App to remove on-device data
  • Disable ad tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking
  • Contact us to request deletion of any server-side data if cloud features are added later

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

Contact

Questions: support@khizermahmud.com or our Support page.
